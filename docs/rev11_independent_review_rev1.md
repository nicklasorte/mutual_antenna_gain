## Summary

- **10 new findings:** 0 High, 4 Medium, 4 Low, 2 Info.
- **U1 to U11, T1, S2, and Q2:** 10 fixed, 4 partly fixed (U1, U2, U9, U10).

**Verdict.** The arithmetic of revision 11 is sound. These all recompute exactly from the CSVs: Table 9-6, Table 9-4's new row, every duration in the summary, Section 9.3, and Appendix B.1, the margin results (56, 105, and 106 volumes; 54, 55, and 105; 44, 47, and 50), and the U1 trial (30 high volumes over 88, k = 3, censored). The rerun reproduces every revision 10 run and 5 dB episode column (largest difference 0). The null table reproduces exactly in MATLAB. The periodic-run definition, the duration (h − 1)k + 1, and the censoring rule are sound.

Four claims don't hold as written:

- **V1 (Medium).** The withdrawal of "a lock can hold the coupling low" goes too far. The claim behind it, "every trial with m4 below 1° has a periodic run of 25 volumes or more", holds only at 0.5° and 60 dBi. On a horizontal path, the two U1 trials (m4 = 0.014° and 0.12°) have no volume at 60 dBi in 24 hours. At 0.5° and 70 dBi they have none either. A lock holds the alignment. Whether that alignment couples high depends on the path and the level.
- **V2 (Medium).** The long periodic runs at m4 of 40° to 60° are explained: they are locks over 6 to 16 volumes. Open item 11 says they "aren't explained". The 23-hour VCP 34 run has a slip of −159.997° per volume, a 9-volume lock that drifts 0.025° per 9 volumes, tighter than any m4 lock in the study. So the guidance to compute the chance of long recurrence from locks over one to four volumes misses most of these runs.
- **V3 (Medium).** The summary's "high coupling recurring every three or four volumes lasts up to 15 hours for VCP 212 and 23 hours for VCP 34" is true but reads as heavier exposure than average. The guaranteed rate inside those runs (2.6 and 1.9 high volumes per hour) is below each mode's average rate (3.14 and 2.74 per hour, Table 9-2). The runs show regular timing, not more frequent coupling.
- **V4 (Medium).** The chance model uses each seed's pooled share of high volumes, not "each trial's own share" as Section 9.3 says. "Ten times" and "22 times" each rest on one null trial in 200 (95% interval 0.01% to 2.75%). The qualitative result holds: long periodic runs exceed chance. The ratios don't: with spread between trials, the VCP 34 ratio could be anywhere from about 5 to about 35.
