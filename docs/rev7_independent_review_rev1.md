# Independent review of the revision 7 working paper

Subject: `docs/mutual_antenna_gain_methodology_report_rev7.md`, commit `c1ef958`. Reviewed November 11, 2026, by a separate agent that did not write revision 7 or see its drafting (red-team layer 2). The reviewer read the code, recomputed numbers from `docs/figures` in Python, and ran the identical code in MATLAB R2025b. It edited and committed nothing.

## Summary

- **14 findings:** 2 High, 6 Medium, 4 Low, 2 Info.
- **The CDF results stand.** Every percentile in Sections 8 and 11 matches its source, and the pattern arithmetic, the bisection search, the batched time series and the exceedance counts all held.
- **The time-series results in Section 9 don't stand as worst-case results.** They were run only at the mode that is worst for the CDF. VCP 35 SAILS×1 (slow clear-air rotation) is worse for the per-volume and harm measures: 44.8% of volumes reach 60 dBi at a 0.5° path, against 30.9%, and 7.4% of 0.5° cuts have a radial hit at 60 dBi, against 3.1% (Q1).
- **The run-length conclusions rest on one seed per case.** With seven seeds of the same model, the longest run ranged from 4 to 22 volumes (about 130 minutes); about half the high-coupling volumes are in runs of two or more (Q2).
- **The code has one silent wrong answer.** `nexrad_asr11_mag_rev1` computes its on-path reference levels at the lowest cut only, so a supplemental low cut makes main beam to main beam come out 72.5 dBi instead of 79.4 dBi (Q6).
- **Several claims need fixing:** the robustness range (Q3), the NOAA-case range (Q4), the ASR-9 mechanism at 0.5° (Q8), and five evidence files that no script produces (Q7).

## Verdict (reviewer)


The CDF numbers in revision 7 hold up. Every value in Tables 8-1, 8-3, 9-1, 9-2 and 11-1 and in Appendix B.1 matches its CSV with consistent half-up rounding. The bisection search, the batched time series and the `histcounts` exceedance give the same answers as the slower code in my independent runs. The pattern-level explanations for the 50%, 99.9%, 99.99% and 99.999% values add up. The weak part is Section 9 and the summary bullets built on it, which are the time-series statistics. They were run only at the mode that is worst for the CDF (VCP 212 SAILS×3 AVSET 6.4), and they are not worst for the per-volume and per-cut metrics. For example, VCP 35 SAILS×1 more than doubles the harm metric and raises the share of volumes reaching 60 dBi from 30.9% to 44.8% at a 0.5° path. The run-length conclusions rest on one seed per configuration. With seven seeds of the same model, the longest run ranged from 4 to 22 volumes (22 volumes is about 130 min). The share of volumes reaching 60 dBi varied by 7 percentage points, against a claimed robustness of "within 3 percentage points". About half of the volumes with high coupling fall in runs of two or more volumes, which "rarely for long" hides. Smaller defects:

- The NOAA-case range "1.7 to 3 dB" is wrong at 99%.
- The ASR-9 0.5° mechanism is explained wrongly.
- The wrapper's on-path reference level is silently wrong when a supplemental cut is added.
- Five of the evidence CSVs, including the NOAA and worked-example tables and the bootstrap, have no script in the repository that produces them.

None of these findings change a CDF percentile in Sections 8 or 11. They do change what the paper can claim about persistence and harm.

## Claims tested that held

- **Bisection equals the full search.** On 434 pairs (random, grid nodes and same-angle ties) × 9 percentiles × 4 cases (ROC, ROC − 6 dB, NSSL, ASR-9): largest percentile difference 0, worst-mode mismatches 0 of 3,906 for each case. The worst-mode rule (first mode pair above the limit one bin lower) matches `max(q_mode)` first-index semantics.
- **Batch equals single.** Seven pairs with mixed takeoff angles (−0.9° to 2.7°, duplicates included), batch size 3: every per-scan table, persistence table, exceedance curve, radial table and floor matched `isequaln`.
- **Exceedance through `histcounts`** equals a direct `mean(x >= g)` to 3.6e-15.
- **Censored-run counting.** By reading the code: a run that touches the first or last complete scan of each trial is counted.
- **`ts_floor_rev1` with vector angles.** Floors of 26.22 dBi (ROC) and 15.44 dBi (NSSL) at 0/0 match Section 9.1.
- **Exact CDF is the default; `nexrad_sl_margin_dB`** is limited to `roc_spec` and to 0 to 20 dB and lowers both envelope levels.
- **The red-team runner's `inputs = "rev6"` takes effect.** The D2 rows differ from Rev 5 at 40 to 50 dBi and are identical at 70 dBi, as the report says.
- **Every table value in Sections 8, 9 and 11.4 and in Appendix B.1** matches its CSV (programmatic check). The 1° and 0.5° radial counts in Section 9.4 match `harm_rev6.csv`.
- **The headline 120 h run reproduces exactly:** 23.736% and 14.170% of volumes at 60 and 70 dBi.
- **The pattern arithmetic holds.**
  - 50% = 5.5 + 3.5 = 9.0 dBi, and −9.5 + 3.5 = −6.0 dBi for NSSL.
  - 99.99% = 34 + 16.5 = 50.5 dBi; it falls to the 42.0 + 3.5 = 45.5 dBi plateau with a 6 dB margin.
  - The 99.9% value sits under the WSR-88D main beam + remote plateau (45.5 and 49.0 dBi).
  - 99.999% does not depend on the pattern.
- **A horizontal path is not worst**, and the maximum over −1° to 3° is a true maximum (nothing higher down to −3° at the ATC end).
- **The envelope bounds any mix of whole volumes for the CDF.** Exceedance is linear in the mode weights. This does not extend to the time-series metrics (Q1).
- **`test_regression_rev6` passes.** Unverified: that the pinned references were written before any Rev 7 change. They were added in the same commit, and I did not rerun commit `a33e806`.
- **Bootstrap method.** It resamples whole trials, the right unit under the per-trial rate model.
